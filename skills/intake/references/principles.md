# Why these intake rules are general

- **Decide engineering and record it.** General because implementation policies can be derived from constraints across products; only the person's goals and circumstances require their answer.
- **Start with the current situation.** General because a new product must improve something the person actually does, and solution preferences alone do not establish that need.
- **Ask permissions at ship.** General because approval needs a concrete action, scope, cost and evidence, which an early product interview cannot yet supply.
- **Count every question.** General because attention is limited regardless of the tool, message or bundled prompt used; a separate confirmation checks the resulting agreement.
- **Preserve the requested outcome.** General because a convenient default is not useful if it removes the user's goal; a documented constraint must be quoted so the conflict is visible.
- **Bound reader passes.** General because review can keep finding new gaps without converging; a hard pass cap brings remaining product assumptions and known gaps to confirmation.
